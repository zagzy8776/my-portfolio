The photographs in `public/playground/` are sourced from Wikimedia Commons and
are public domain, CC0, or Creative Commons licences that permit commercial use
and redistribution. Each image is colour-graded into the site palette at build
time; grading does not alter the licence terms, and attribution is required for
the CC-BY and CC-BY-SA files listed below.

Regenerate with `python scripts/fetch-journal-photos.py`.
